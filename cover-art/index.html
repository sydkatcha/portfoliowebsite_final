THIS IS WATER
small book cover design // 2026
“This, I submit, is the freedom of a real education, of learning how to be well-adjusted. You get to consciously decide what has meaning and what doesn't.”
This is a format design project featuring the graduation speech "This Is Water" by David Foster Wallace. The Swiss inspired design is used to convey Wallace's simple language, sarcasm, and strong message. Purposefully rotated text, shapes, and single question mark encourage the viewer to look beyond the obvious and question how they perceive the world.
FLOWERS FOR ALGERNON
book cover design // 2025
"Intelligence without the ability to give and receive affection leads to mental and moral breakdown."
Designing this cover, I was inspired by the Rorschach inkblot cards, by which the protagonist's neurological changes are occasionally measured in the story. The cover features a muted, cool-toned color scheme, and the illustrations nod to the protagonist's mouse companion, Algernon, who is also subjected to the same tests alongside him.
NEXT TO NORMAL
album cover design // 2025
“The price of love is loss, but still we pay.”
Challenge : create a visually balanced album design that feels gritty, nods to pharmaceuticals, instability, and grief, all while still having a “broadway musical aesthetic.”

This album cover design features the 2009 Original Broadway Cast Recording album Next to Normal, which follows a suburban family struggling with the loss of a son and the challenges of mental healthcare and psychopharmacology. The musical's songs lean towards the rock genre.
