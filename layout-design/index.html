BOULDERS, BREWS, & BANANAS
t-shirt + poster design // 2026
This project focused on advertising a Bouldering Competition at Boulders & Brews Climbing + Coffee in Fayetteville, AR. The competition was minion-themed, but the advertising could not include copyrighted minion content. As such, I took a typographic route and hand-made my own minion-language stamp which I printed on paper and manipulated in Illustrator.
