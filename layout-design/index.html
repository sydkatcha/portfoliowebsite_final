t-shirt + poster design // 2026
BOULDERS, BREWS & BANANAS
This project focused on advertising a Bouldering Competition at Boulders & Brews Climbing + Coffee in Fayetteville, AR. The competition was minion-themed, but the advertising could not include copyrighted minion content. As such, I took a typographic route and hand-made my own minion-language stamp which I printed on paper and manipulated in Illustrator.
small book design // 2025
ODE TO BREAD
This project pairs Ode to Bread (Oda al Pan, 1954)—a poem written by Pablo Neruda from his collection Odas elementales—with experimental photography that explores light, shadow, texture, and color. Bread is hallowed, sacred, deep, simple, pure, and full— yet hidden, denied, spoiled, a symbol of the rich man's greed.
menu redesign // 2026
LITTLE BREAD CO
This project was a redesign of a local Fayetteville cafe menu so that it had a unique, eclectic style and could be read on a single page with appropriate hierarchy. I also chose to make the menu available in three different color varieties to mirror the cafe's colorful, vibrant atmosphere.
book design // 2025
ACTS & ALLEGORIES
Acts and Allegories was a multi-month class project I led as project manager alongside design manager Cara Spratlin. The 8x10in, 266 pg book recounts Aesop's fables through the lens of historical circus photography of the 1850s-1950s.It divides Aesop's fables into 5 distinct chapters: the ringmaster, the beast, the performer, the worker, and the trickster, each representing Aesop's respective moral message groups: power and authority, true nature and instinct, pride and hubris, hard work and resilience, and deception and wit. The book's design heavily focuses on specific colors, textures, typefaces, and metaphors that compliment each chapter's message.
